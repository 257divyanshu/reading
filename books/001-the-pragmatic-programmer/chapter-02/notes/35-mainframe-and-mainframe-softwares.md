## 🔹 Mainframe software

👉 Mainframe software = software running on a **mainframe computer**

A **mainframe** is:

* a very large, powerful enterprise computer
* designed for:

  * huge transaction processing
  * reliability
  * supporting thousands/millions of users

---

# 🔹 Used by

Typically:

* banks
* airlines
* insurance companies
* governments
* telecom companies

---

# 🔹 Example tasks

A bank mainframe may handle:

* ATM transactions
* account balances
* credit card processing

---

# 🔹 Important characteristic

Many mainframe systems are:

* **very old**
* written decades ago
* still critical

Often in:

* COBOL
* FORTRAN
* PL/I

---

# 🔹 Why authors mentioned it

Because companies often need to:

* integrate modern apps
  with
* old mainframe systems

But those old systems:

* may not have APIs
* may only expose terminal screens

So developers use:

* screen scraping
* scripting
* mini-languages

---

# 🔹 What mainframe apps often look like

Usually text-terminal interfaces like:

```text id="jrb8dr"
ENTER ACCOUNT NUMBER:
> _
```

instead of modern GUIs.

---

# 🔹 One-line takeaway

👉 **Mainframe software = large-scale legacy enterprise software running on powerful centralized computers, often still used in critical industries today.**
